## The question

During extreme floods, erosion and sediment transport can cause much of the damage. How can repeated, high-resolution topography help identify where those changes are most likely to occur?

## The approach

The project combines Digital Surface Models derived from Pléiades Neo and CO3D imagery with efficient hydrodynamic simulations. It will quantify hydraulic and morphological indicators across flood-event time series, beginning with landscapes affected by the Kaikōura earthquake and subsequent floods.

## Why it matters

The aim is to identify topographic areas susceptible to flood-related morphological damage and improve our understanding of how pre-existing topography controls fluvial response from local to regional scales.
