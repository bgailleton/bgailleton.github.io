Compress to JPG

Supports formats your browser can open, such as JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, AVIF, and SVG. HEIC, TIFF, and RAW support depends on your browser. Transparent areas become white; animations become a still image.

Dimensions

Leave blank to keep the original dimensions. Aspect ratio is preserved; images are never enlarged.

JPG quality

Uses the highest quality within this range that fits. Set both values equal for a fixed quality. Higher minimum quality may require smaller dimensions.

If your constraints cannot all be met, increase the file size limit, lower the minimum quality, or allow resizing. Very large images are limited to 8,192 px per side and 24 megapixels. 1 KB = 1,000 bytes.

For a batch, the same settings apply to every image. Download the successful JPGs together as a ZIP; any files that cannot be processed are listed below.
